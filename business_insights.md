# Business Insights

This section is intended to be completed after running the analysis queries in MySQL.

## Areas to report

### Customer Intelligence
- Customer segment distribution
- Highest-value customers by successful transaction value
- High-activity customers
- Customers performing above their segment average

### Transaction Intelligence
- Total successful transaction value
- Dominant transaction types
- Most-used transaction channels
- Monthly transaction trends
- Month-over-month growth
- Top customers by transaction contribution

### Loan Intelligence
- Total loan exposure
- Highest-value loan types
- Active vs closed vs defaulted loans
- Customers with late/missed payments
- High loan exposure customers

### Credit Card Intelligence
- Highest credit utilization
- Customers above 50% utilization
- Blocked card exposure

### Support Intelligence
- Most common ticket categories
- High-priority tickets
- Average satisfaction score
- Average resolution time
- Open/in-progress tickets requiring attention

## Recommended final insight format

1. **Finding:** State the result.
2. **Evidence:** Mention the SQL metric/query used.
3. **Business Impact:** Explain why it matters.
4. **Recommendation:** Suggest a practical action.
